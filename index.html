Online check-in: students use their own phones

Connect a small service (a Google Sheet plus a free Google script that you own). Once connected, each student’s QR code opens a page on their phone where they confirm their attendance, and the check-in appears on this dashboard within seconds. Connect it before you generate or send QR codes, because the QR code content changes once the service is connected.

Set-up steps (once, about 5 minutes)
  1. Click “Download service code”. Your admin key is filled in for you.
  2. Create a new Google Sheet (sheets.new), then Extensions → Apps Script. Delete the sample code, paste in the contents of the downloaded file, and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app. Set “Execute as” to Me and “Who has access” to Anyone. Deploy, then allow the permissions. Google’s “not verified” notice is normal for your own script (Advanced → Go to …).
  4. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it below, and click “Test connection”.

Sessions and QR codes

Each session gives every student a different QR code that is linked to the course and session number you pick. When a code is scanned, the student is recorded under that course (see “Course attendance from scans” above) and their semester count goes up by 1. With the online service connected, students scan their own code with their phone camera and confirm; their check-ins arrive here automatically. The lecturer’s scanner below keeps working too.

Send QR codes to students

Each button prepares that student’s own message using the LBS email or phone number from the student CSV. Email downloads the QR image and opens a draft, so attach the downloaded PNG before sending. WhatsApp and SMS open a ready message (send the downloaded PNG in the chat). On a phone, Share attaches the image directly. Nothing is sent automatically.
